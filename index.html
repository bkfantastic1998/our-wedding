Our Story

We met online — two strangers behind glowing screens, not knowing that a simple message would change everything. Eleven years later, here we are: still choosing each other, every single day. We've cried together and laughed until we couldn't breathe. We've faced difficulties that could have broken us and solved problems that once seemed impossible. Distance kept us apart, but it never kept us from loving each other — every holiday, every reunion, was a homecoming. And somewhere in between, we walked together in the rain, hand in hand, letting the world blur around us. Now we're ready to say "I do" — not as two people meeting for the first time, but as two people who have already lived a lifetime of love. We can't wait to celebrate with the people we love most.
